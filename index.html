We engineer our own beryllium composite drivers instead of sourcing off-the-shelf components. Every Sonix headphone goes through 120 hours of acoustic burn-in and a 47-point quality check before it ships. You are not paying for a logo. You are paying for a decade of acoustic research packed into a pair of ear cups.

Buy with complete confidence. If you are not satisfied within 30 days of delivery, email us and we will arrange a free collection from your door. No re-stocking fees, no questions, no friction. Refund hits your account within 3 business days of us receiving the unit.

Every Sonix headphone works with any Bluetooth device out of the box, including iOS, Android, Windows, and macOS. The free Sonix app (iOS and Android) unlocks advanced EQ presets, ANC intensity control, and firmware updates, but it is never required for core functionality. We believe your hardware should work perfectly without an app.

2-year international warranty covers all manufacturing defects, driver failure, and electronic faults. Ear cushions and cables are covered for 1 year. Accidental damage (drops, liquid) is not covered by the standard warranty but can be added via our optional Sonix Shield plan at checkout for a small annual fee.

Orders over $200 ship free worldwide. Standard delivery is 3 to 5 business days within the US and EU, and 5 to 10 days internationally. Express next-day shipping is available at checkout for most regions. Every order ships with full tracking from the moment it leaves our warehouse in Zurich.

The Apex Pro (open-back) and Studio M1 (closed-back) are designed specifically for professional monitoring. Both include a detachable 6.35mm balanced cable, a hardshell carrying case, and a flat frequency response certified to within 1dB of the stated curve. Multiple Grammy-winning engineers use Sonix as their primary reference headphone.
